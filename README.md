# NutriCart

NutriCart is an adaptive nutrition and grocery planning application designed for Indian users with common health conditions such as Type 2 Diabetes and hypertension.

Instead of forcing unrealistic diet plans, NutriCart re-engineers familiar Indian meals into healthier alternatives while respecting cultural food habits, affordability, and lifestyle constraints.

---

# Problem Statement

Many nutrition apps recommend generic diets that:
- ignore Indian eating habits
- are expensive or difficult to maintain
- replace familiar foods completely
- fail to consider long-term sustainability

People often abandon such diets because they feel disconnected from their everyday lives.

NutriCart addresses this by adapting familiar meals into healthier versions rather than replacing them entirely.

---

# Key Idea

NutriCart focuses on:

- Familiar Indian meals
- Health-aware substitutions
- Budget-conscious meal planning
- Pantry-aware grocery suggestions
- Personalized adaptive nutrition

Example adaptations:
- White rice dosa → Ragi dosa
- Fried snacks → Roasted sundal
- High sodium chutneys → Mint peanut chutney
- Refined rice meals → Brown + white rice combinations
